## Step 1: Growing the Perfect Crop to Carve a Pumpkin

To carve a pumpkin, you first need to cultivate a healthy supply during the Fall season. Pumpkin seeds take a full 13 days to mature without fertilizers, making them one of the slowest-growing crops in the valley. Careful planning during the first week of autumn guarantees you have plenty of raw materials ready well before the local festival.

Pierre sells Pumpkin Seeds at the general store for 100 gold, while JojaMart sells them for 125 gold (or 100 gold after completing the Joja Community Development project). If you plant on Fall 1, your first harvest arrives on Fall 14. A second planting on Fall 14 matures on Fall 27, just in time for the Spirit's Eve celebration.

Using fertilizers alters the timeline and quality distribution. While any crop quality works when you carve a pumpkin into decorative items, saving basic-quality harvests for crafting and selling gold or iridium varieties preserves maximum profit.

Community reports highlight the excitement of growing Giant Crops. If you plant pumpkins in a contiguous 3x3 grid, they hold a 1% daily chance to fuse into a single giant crop once fully grown. While you cannot directly carve a pumpkin giant into a mega-lantern, cracking it with an axe yields 15 to 21 individual items, stocking your chests with crafting supplies.

## Crafting the Jack-O'-Lantern: How to Carve a Pumpkin Step-by-Step

The primary way to carve a pumpkin into ambient lighting is by crafting the classic Jack-O'-Lantern. Unlike standard farm recipes learned through leveling your Farming skill, this blueprint requires an annual festival visit.

### Unlocking the Blueprint

Make your way into Pelican Town on the evening of Fall 27 between 10:00 PM and 11:50 PM to attend the Spirit's Eve festival. Pierre sets up a seasonal shop near the town center where he sells rare goods. 

You must purchase the Jack-O'-Lantern recipe for 750 gold. Once bought, the recipe permanently enters your crafting menu under the lighting tab, allowing you to produce as many lanterns as you desire in future seasons.

### Required Materials and Assembly

Once you unlock the blueprint, crafting requires only two basic ingredients:
- **1 Pumpkin** (Any quality level)
- **1 Torch** (Crafted from 1 Wood and 2 Sap)

Open your crafting menu, select the glowing Jack-O'-Lantern icon, and the item drops straight into your inventory. You can place these lanterns anywhere on your farm, inside barns, along Pelican Town pathways, or inside sheds to establish a spooky aesthetic.

| **Torch Sourcing** | 1 Wood + 2 Sap | Collect sap throughout spring and summer clear-cutting |

## The Wearable Look: Tailoring a Pumpkin Mask

Farm decoration is only half the experience. You can also carve a pumpkin aesthetic into your character's wardrobe by tailoring a festive helmet known as the Pumpkin Mask. This carved hollow pumpkin fits directly over your farmer's head, complete with glowing triangular eyeholes.

To fabricate this costume piece, you must gain access to Emily's sewing machine at 2 Willow Lane. The sewing station unlocks the morning after acquiring your first bolt of Cloth, which can be spun in a Loom using Sheep or Rabbit Wool, or recovered as a drop from Mummies in the Skull Cavern.

Follow these simple tailoring steps:
1. Walk over to the sewing machine inside Emily and Haley's home.
2. Place a piece of **Cloth** into the sewing machine's spool slot (left side).
3. Place an unmodified **Pumpkin** into the top-right feed slot.
4. Press the power button on the sewing console to complete the stitch.

The resulting Pumpkin Mask can be equipped in your primary hat slot. It offers no defensive combat stats, but player experience shows it pairs wonderfully with dark clothes during fall multiplayer events.

## Avoiding the Winter Rot: Protecting Your Carved Creations

One critical mechanic catches unsuspecting farmers off guard every year: Jack-O'-Lanterns rot at the turn of the season. When Fall 28 transitions into Winter 1, every Jack-O'-Lantern positioned outside on your farmland automatically transforms into an unsightly **Rotten Plant**.

A Rotten Plant emits zero light and clutters your pathways. While you can quickly clear away the debris using a scythe, pickaxe, or axe, your hard-earned pumpkins are permanently lost.

To preserve your work:
- **Move Them Indoors:** Jack-O'-Lanterns placed inside the Farmhouse, Farm Cabins, Greenhouses, or Sheds do not decay. They maintain their glowing orange appearance year-round.
- **Chest Storage:** Pick up outdoor lanterns on Fall 28 using an empty tool slot or pickaxe, then store them safely in a storage chest until the following autumn.
- **Harvesting Seeds:** If you accidentally allow an uncarved crop to sit in a chest, you can run it through a Seed Maker during winter to prepare a seed surplus for next year.

Taking ten minutes at the end of Fall 28 to retrieve your outdoor lights ensures you never have to carve a pumpkin replacement collection from scratch when spring arrives.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you carve a pumpkin before attending Spirit's Eve in Year 1?
No, you cannot craft a decorative Jack-O'-Lantern before Spirit's Eve because Pierre only sells the crafting recipe during the festival on Fall 27. However, if you already have access to Emily's sewing machine and Cloth, you can use a sewing machine to carve a pumpkin into a wearable Pumpkin Mask earlier in the month.

### Do quality stars affect the light radius when you carve a pumpkin?
No. Using an Iridium, Gold, or Silver quality pumpkin produces the exact same Jack-O'-Lantern as a basic-quality crop. The light intensity, radius, and sell value of the crafted light remain identical. It is best to sell high-quality produce and save basic crops for crafting.

### What happens if I leave my carved Jack-O'-Lanterns outside on Winter 1?
Any Jack-O'-Lantern left outdoors on your farm or around town will decay overnight into a Rotten Plant on Winter 1. To save your decorations, pick them up on Fall 28 and store them in a chest or display them inside your farmhouse or shed where winter weather cannot damage them.
